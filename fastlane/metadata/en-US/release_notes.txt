First public release of FrameMate for Mac.

Includes:
- Full-display and single-window recording
- Camera, screen + camera, and audio-only modes
- Microphone, optional system audio, cursor highlights, and pause/resume
- Countdown, duration, end-warning, audio, window, and output-folder settings
- Frame Coach, automatic reframing, and spoken guidance
- VoiceOver-friendly controls, keyboard shortcuts, and live announcements
