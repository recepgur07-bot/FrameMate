Record tutorials, product demos, lessons, presentations, and clear narrated explanations on your Mac. FrameMate is an accessibility-first local Mac recorder that brings screen, camera, and audio into one focused workflow.

WHAT YOU CAN RECORD

• SCREEN AND WINDOW RECORDING
Capture your full display or a single window as a 1080p MP4. Use it for lessons, onboarding, product walkthroughs, and presentations.

• SCREEN + CAMERA
Place your camera over the screen. Choose the camera, then set the overlay position and size to make your explanation more personal.

• CAMERA OR AUDIO
Record standalone camera videos, microphone audio, system audio, or both. Choose automatic, mono, or stereo microphone channels.

TOOLS FOR CLEAR EXPLANATIONS

• Add microphone and optional system audio to screen recordings, with separate volume controls.
• Highlight the cursor and clicks so viewers can follow every step.
• Pause and resume without losing the recording timeline.
• Open the finished file, rename it, reveal it in Finder, or copy it elsewhere with Save As.

FRAME COACH

Frame Coach gives spoken guidance to help keep your face in frame. Adjust automatic reframing, directional tones, center confirmation, on-screen text, feedback frequency, and repeat interval. It is designed for consistent camera setup without constantly checking the preview.

SETTINGS FOR YOUR WORKFLOW

• Choose a countdown, unlimited recording, a maximum duration, a custom 5-second-to-60-minute limit, and an end-of-recording warning.
• Choose recording start, stop, pause, and resume sounds, plus elapsed-time announcements and their frequency.
• Hide the app window when recording starts, bring it back when recording stops, run in the Dock or menu bar, and launch at login.
• Choose the default recording folder. Your latest recording setup is remembered for the next session.
• Use optional keyboard shortcuts, Quick Help, and direct Help, Privacy, and Terms links from Settings.

ACCESSIBILITY

FrameMate is designed with VoiceOver and keyboard use in mind. Recording start, pause, error, and completion states are announced live. Frame Coach provides spoken framing feedback for blind and low-vision creators. The optional global-shortcut feature requests macOS Accessibility permission only when enabled; the core recording workflow does not require it.

PRIVACY

Recordings are processed and saved locally on your Mac. FrameMate requires no account, cloud upload, advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

ACCESS AND PRO

Start without an account. The first 3 days include unlimited local recording; after that, every user receives 7 free recording minutes each month. Choose a yearly auto-renewable subscription or a one-time lifetime Pro purchase whenever you need unlimited recording. Restore Purchases is included.
