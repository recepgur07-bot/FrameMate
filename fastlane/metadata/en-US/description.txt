Record polished tutorials, demos, lessons, and presentations on your Mac without a complicated setup.

FrameMate is a native Mac recorder designed for creators, teachers, and teams who need clean recordings with clear narration and accessibility support.

WHAT YOU CAN RECORD

• SCREEN AND WINDOW RECORDING
Capture your full screen or a single window as an MP4. Great for product walkthroughs, lessons, onboarding, and tutorials.

• SCREEN + CAMERA
Add your camera as an overlay to make your presentation more engaging.

• CAMERA OR AUDIO ONLY
Record standalone camera videos for quick updates, or capture microphone audio for voice notes and podcast drafts.

TOOLS FOR CLEAR EXPLANATIONS
• Optional system audio for screen recordings
• Cursor highlight overlays to make clicks visible
• Simple pause and resume controls

FRAME COACH
Frame Coach gives live spoken guidance to keep your face centered. It is especially useful for creators who want consistent camera framing without checking the screen constantly.

ACCESSIBILITY FOR BLIND AND LOW-VISION CREATORS
FrameMate is built from the ground up with VoiceOver support:
• Keyboard-friendly controls
• Live status announcements for recordings and errors
• Frame Coach guidance makes it possible to set up your shot independently without visual feedback

PRIVACY
Your recordings are processed and saved locally on your Mac. FrameMate does not require an account, does not upload videos, and includes no tracking SDKs.
