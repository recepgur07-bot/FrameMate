FRAME COACH

Frame Coach gives live spoken guidance to help keep your face centered and well lit while recording. It is especially useful for creators who want more consistent camera framing without checking the preview constantly.

ACCESSIBILITY FOR BLIND AND LOW-VISION CREATORS

FrameMate is especially strong for blind and low-vision users who need spoken recording feedback. VoiceOver support, keyboard-friendly controls, live status announcements, and Frame Coach guidance make it easier to record without relying on constant visual preview checks.
