FrameMate is a native macOS screen, camera, and audio recorder for local recording workflows, with concrete accessibility support for blind and low-vision creators.

No account is required. Recordings are processed and saved on the user's Mac. The app does not upload recordings and includes no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Permissions used by the app:

- Camera: camera-only recording, screen + camera overlay, and Frame Coach guidance.
- Microphone: microphone narration in video recordings and audio-only recording.
- Screen Recording: full-display and single-window capture, plus system audio where available. If permission is granted while FrameMate is already open, macOS may briefly require the app to return to the foreground or be relaunched because of TCC caching.
- Accessibility (AX): requested only when the optional global Keyboard Shortcuts feature is enabled. It is off by default and is not required for the core recording workflow. VoiceOver support itself uses standard macOS accessibility APIs and needs no additional permission.
- Speech: spoken Frame Coach guidance.

Modes and settings:

- The main workflow offers horizontal camera, horizontal screen, and audio-only modes. Screen recording can target a full display or one window, and can include microphone audio, system audio, cursor highlights, and a camera overlay with selectable camera, position, and size.
- Camera mode supports automatic reframing. Frame Coach offers automatic, VoiceOver, app-voice, or silent guidance, directional tones, center confirmation, on-screen text, feedback frequency, and repeat interval.
- Recording settings include countdown, unlimited or capped duration, custom 5-second-to-60-minute duration, end warning, automatic/mono/stereo microphone channels, event sounds, and elapsed-time announcements.
- Workflow settings can hide/show the window around recording, run in the Dock or only the menu bar, launch at login, choose the output folder, and use keyboard shortcuts for modes, settings announcements, start/stop, and pause/resume.
- Settings also contains Quick Help, Help & Support, Privacy Policy, Apple's standard EULA, purchase controls, and Restore Purchases.

Recording storage (Guideline 2.4.5(i)): recordings are saved to the user-visible ~/Movies/Video Recorder folder by default using the com.apple.security.assets.movies.read-write entitlement, never to a hidden app container. Users can change the folder with a standard picker, and each completed recording offers Open, rename, Save As, and Show in Finder. Custom folders use security-scoped bookmarks. The default folder is named Video Recorder because it follows the bundle name; the marketing name is FrameMate.

Access model: no account or purchase is required to start recording. New users get 3 days of unlimited local recording, then a device-only quota of 7 free recording minutes per calendar month. This access is tracked on-device and is not a StoreKit trial. FrameMate Pro offers a yearly auto-renewable subscription and a lifetime one-time purchase for unlimited recording, from the paywall or Settings. The paywall includes Restore Purchases, the Privacy Policy, and Apple's standard EULA. There is no hidden tester unlock; TestFlight and review builds require active local access, remaining free quota, or a purchase.

Suggested reviewer flow:
1. Launch FrameMate and complete onboarding.
2. Grant only the permissions needed for the mode being tested.
3. Test a short camera, screen, or audio recording and open the resulting file.
4. Open Settings to review recording, Frame Coach, accessibility, output-folder, purchase, restore, and privacy/support controls.
5. Open Quick Help and try the keyboard shortcuts if the optional Accessibility permission is enabled.
