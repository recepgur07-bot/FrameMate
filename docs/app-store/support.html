FrameMate Help Center

Record clearly. Navigate confidently.

FrameMate is a Mac recording app for screen capture, screen + camera videos, camera framing support, and audio recording. This page explains the app's modes, permissions, settings, keyboard shortcuts, accessibility features, purchases, and troubleshooting in both English and Turkish.

English Guide

A practical guide for first-time setup, daily use, and accessibility-first recording.

Quick Start

1. Open FrameMate and complete the onboarding permission steps for the features you plan to use.
2. Choose a recording mode: camera, screen, or audio.
3. Select your camera, microphone, screen, or window if the mode requires it.
4. Adjust options like system audio, cursor highlight, keyboard shortcut overlay, or camera box.
5. Start recording from the main button, the app menu, or the global shortcut Cmd+Option+R.

Recording Modes

  • Horizontal Camera Recording: records your camera feed for talking-head videos and works with Frame Coach and auto reframing.
  • Horizontal Screen Recording: records a full display or a single window. You can add microphone audio, system audio, cursor highlight, shortcut overlay, and an optional camera box.
  • Audio Recording: records microphone audio, system audio, or both.

Permissions

  • Camera: required for camera recording and for the screen camera box overlay.
  • Microphone: required whenever microphone audio is part of the recording.
  • Screen Recording: required for full-screen capture, window capture, and system audio capture on macOS.
  • Accessibility: helpful for keyboard shortcut overlays in exported screen videos and for broader keyboard-friendly workflows.

If you grant Screen Recording permission, macOS may require quitting and reopening FrameMate before the permission becomes active.

Main Recording Options

Screen Source Choose whether to capture a full display or a single shareable window.
Microphone Select the input device or leave it off in screen mode if you only want system audio.
System Audio Adds sound from apps and macOS to the recording.
Cursor Highlight Adds a soft pointer emphasis and click ring in exported screen recordings.
Show Keyboard Shortcuts Briefly displays meaningful shortcuts such as Command, Control, Option, and similar key combinations in the video.
Camera Box Adds your camera on top of a screen recording. You can choose the camera, position, and size.
Auto Reframe For single-person camera recordings, software adjusts framing to keep the shot more balanced.

Frame Coach and Accessibility

  • Frame Coach provides spoken guidance to help keep your face centered and usable without constantly checking the preview.
  • It is especially helpful for blind and low-vision creators because the app supports VoiceOver, keyboard navigation, live announcements, and spoken recording guidance.
  • Speech Mode can switch between automatic, spoken app feedback, or silence.
  • Spatial Audio Mode can send guidance directionally so left and right cues are easier to follow with headphones.
  • Center Confirmation plays a short confirmation sound when your face is centered.
  • Feedback Frequency and Repeat Interval control how often coaching messages repeat.
  • Show Guidance Text On Screen keeps guidance visible as text during recording if you want visual reinforcement.

Settings

  • Countdown: set how many seconds FrameMate waits before recording starts.
  • Maximum Recording Duration: stops the recording automatically after the chosen limit.
  • Hide Window When Recording Starts: useful for screen recordings so the app window does not appear in the capture.
  • Show Window When Recording Stops: automatically brings FrameMate back after the recording ends.
  • App Appearance: choose whether the app appears in the Dock or runs from the menu bar only.
  • Launch At Login: opens FrameMate automatically when your Mac starts.
  • Default Recording Folder: defines where completed recordings are saved on your Mac.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Cmd+1 Select horizontal camera recording.
Cmd+2 Select horizontal screen recording.
Cmd+3 Select audio recording.
Cmd+D Turn Frame Coach on or off.
Cmd+I Have FrameMate announce the current settings.
Cmd+Option+R Start or stop the main recording globally, even when FrameMate is in the background.
Cmd+Option+5 Start or stop audio-only recording globally.
Cmd+Option+P Pause or resume an active recording.

After Recording

When a recording finishes, FrameMate can open the file, reveal it in Finder, rename it, or save a copy to a different location. Recordings are stored locally on your Mac in the selected output folder.

Purchases

FrameMate offers a yearly plan and a one-time lifetime purchase. If you already bought access, use Restore Purchases in the app settings first. If access still does not appear, contact support.

The app follows Apple's standard end-user license agreement for App Store distribution: Terms of Use.

Troubleshooting

  • If camera, microphone, or screen recording does not work, open System Settings > Privacy & Security and confirm that FrameMate has the needed permissions.
  • If system audio is missing, verify that Screen Recording permission is enabled and restart the app.
  • If keyboard shortcut overlays do not appear, make sure FrameMate is allowed under Accessibility in System Settings.
  • If a file is not where you expected, check the default recording folder in Settings and confirm that enough disk space is available.

Turkce Kilavuz

Ilk kurulum, gunluk kullanim ve erisilebilirlik odakli kayit icin acik ve pratik rehber.

Hizli Baslangic

1. FrameMate'i ac ve kullanacagin ozelliklere gore onboarding izin adimlarini tamamla.
2. Kayit modunu sec: kamera, ekran ya da ses.
3. Gerekliyse kamera, mikrofon, ekran veya pencere secimini yap.
4. Sistem sesi, imlec vurgusu, klavye kisayolu gosterimi veya kamera kutusu gibi secenekleri ayarla.
5. Kaydi ana dugmeden, uygulama menusunden veya global kisayol Cmd+Option+R ile baslat.

Kayit Modlari

  • Yatay Video Kaydi: kamerani kaydeder; konusmali videolar icin uygundur ve Kadraj Kocu ile otomatik yeniden kadrajlama destekler.
  • Yatay Ekran Kaydi: tam ekran ya da tek bir pencere kaydeder. Mikrofon, sistem sesi, imlec vurgusu, klavye kisayolu gosterimi ve istege bagli kamera kutusu eklenebilir.
  • Ses Kaydi: mikrofonu, sistem sesini veya ikisini birlikte kaydeder.

Izinler

  • Kamera: kamera kaydi ve ekran ustu kamera kutusu icin gerekir.
  • Mikrofon: kayda mikrofon sesi eklenecekse gerekir.
  • Ekran Kaydi: tam ekran, pencere ve sistem sesi kaydi icin macOS tarafinda gerekir.
  • Erisilebilirlik: klavye kisayollarini videoda gostermek ve daha rahat klavye kullanim senaryolari icin faydalidir.

Ekran Kaydi iznini verdikten sonra, iznin aktif olmasi icin FrameMate'i kapatip yeniden acman gerekebilir.

Kadraj Kocu ve Erisilebilirlik

  • Kadraj Kocu, yuzunun kadrajda daha dogru kalmasi icin sesli yonlendirme verir; boylece onizlemeyi surekli kontrol etmen gerekmez.
  • Ozellikle kor ve az goren ureticiler icin gucludur; cunku VoiceOver destegi, klavye ile kullanilabilen kontrol yapisi, canli durum anonslari ve sesli kayit rehberligi birlikte calisir.
  • Yonlendirme Sesi, otomatik mod, uygulama sesi veya sessiz mod arasinda secilebilir.
  • Yon Sesi, kulaklikla kullanirken sag ve sol yonleri daha kolay takip etmek icin yonsel ses kullanabilir.
  • Merkez Onayi, yuzun ortaya geldiginde kisa bir onay sesi calar.
  • Geri Bildirim Sikligi ve Ayni Uyariyi Tekrarla, yonlendirmelerin ne kadar sik verilecegini belirler.
  • Ekranda Yonlendirme Metnini Goster, istersen sesli rehbere ek olarak metni de gorunur tutar.

Ayarlar

  • Geri Sayim Suresi: kayit baslamadan once kac saniye beklenecegini ayarlar.
  • Maksimum Kayit Suresi: secilen sure dolunca kaydi otomatik durdurur.
  • Kayit Baslarken Pencereyi Gizle: ozellikle ekran kaydinda uygulama penceresinin gorunmesini engeller.
  • Kayit Bitince Pencereyi Geri Ac: kayit sonunda FrameMate'i otomatik geri getirir.
  • Uygulama Gorunumu: uygulamanin Dock'ta mi yoksa sadece menu bardan mi calisacagini belirler.
  • Giriste Otomatik Baslat: Mac acildiginda FrameMate'i otomatik acabilir.
  • Varsayilan Kayit Klasoru: kayitlarin Mac'inde hangi klasore kaydedilecegini belirler.

Kayit Sonrasi

Kayit tamamlandiginda FrameMate dosyayi acabilir, Finder'da gosterebilir, yeniden adlandirabilir veya farkli bir konuma kopya kaydedebilir. Kayitlar secili cikti klasorune yerel olarak kaydedilir.

Sorun Giderme
